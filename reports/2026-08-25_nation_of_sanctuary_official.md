## Key Figures (exact from official)
Total net WG expenditure 1 Apr 2019 – 31 Mar 2025: £63.87 million
Of which Ukraine response: £58.22 million (91%)
Non-Ukraine asylum/refugee integration & support: £5.65 million (9%)
Yearly net (£000s):
2019-20: 510
2020-21: 91
2021-22: 655
2022-23: 26,334
2023-24: 31,648
2024-25: 4,635
Cumulative reaches 63,873
