# Report: Gwynedd Temporary/Emergency Accommodation Costs
Date: 2026-08-25
Status: Verified from FOI response and democracy reports
Primary Sources:
- WhatDoTheyKnow FOI Ref 253: https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/temporary_accommodation_328
- Gwynedd democracy.gwynedd.llyw.cymru reports confirming ongoing pressures

## Key Figures
2023/24 actual: £6,793,132.54
2024/25 predicted: around £6.8 million
Historical growth (public FOI series): from under £500k (2018) to £5.6m (2022) then £6.79m (2023/24).
